Guidance

Recommended launch card: Standard Analyze $0.039/min; grounded/dense $0.10–$0.15/min; Search indexing $0.039/min; stored index $0.06/video-hour/month; Search $3/1,000 queries; output text $7.50/M tokens or a clearly stated included allowance.

Landmines / do not say

  • Do not call 1-FPS Molmo processing equivalent to Twelve Labs without side-by-side quality benchmarks.
  • Do not claim Inqox is cheaper while advertising $0.06–$0.08/min as the base Analyze tier.
  • Do not sell Search until the embedding quality, indexing latency, filters, and timestamp retrieval are tested.
  • Do not claim enterprise reliability, audio parity, private deployment, or an SLA before those capabilities exist.
  • Resolve Molmo 2's disclosed training-data licensing caveat before commercial launch.

Sources and evidence posture

  • Twelve Labs pricing — current Developer Search, Analyze, Embed, output-token, free-tier, and capacity terms.

  • Official Molmo 2 repository — video inference, frame/context configuration, and serving support.
